How to play

Tap a jar, then tap another to pour its top layer onto the same fruit or into an empty jar. A jar full of one fruit is sealed with wax. Jars in a brass cradle can be turned upside down: tap the ring to bring the bottom layer to the top. Undo and Restart are always free. Stuck? Use a Hint, add a Fresh Jar, or Peek under the wax paper.

Purchases

All 150 days and the Daily Jar are free. The Picking Basket, Market Hamper and Harvest Wagon add tools; the Endless Cellar unlocks endless jars and past Daily Jars. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
