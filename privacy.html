Privacy Policy

Topsy Larder · last updated October 29, 2026

Topsy Larder is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the days you have solved and your best move counts, Daily Jars played, Endless Cellar progress, and your stock of tools (Hint, Fresh Jar, Peek).
  • Small reminders, such as which one-time tips you have already seen.
  • Whether you have bought the Picking Basket, Market Hamper, Harvest Wagon or Endless Cellar, and the store's transaction ids so a purchase is credited only once.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

All 150 days and the Daily Jar are free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
